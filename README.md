## Synopsis
> After the invasion of Japan by the Brittannian Empire, the country is renamed to "Area 11". The young Lelouch, a Brittannian student living in Japan, accidentally gets caught up in a local terrorist attack against Brittannia, reuniting with his childhood friend Suzaku as well as meeting the mysterious girl C.C. Cornered in a life and death situation, Lelouch accepts a power C.C. offers to him, the ability to order anyone to do anything. Harboring a thirst for revenge against the Brittanian Empire for their actions against his family, Lelouch wages war against the Empire. Using his newfound power and tactical ability, Lelouch goes under the alias Zero in attempts to fulfill his childhood vow, to destroy Brittannia!

**Characters:**

![lelouchpic](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6d4c768d-d724-4f8e-b499-3e8b4a42159d)

### Lelouch ~~Lamperouge~~ vi Brittania
: Driven by a desire for revenge against the Britannian Empire, Lelouch is a brilliant strategist who uses his intellect and newfound power of **Geass** to overthrow the existing order. His charismatic personality and strong convictions inspire others to follow him, but his ruthless methods and willingness to sacrifice allies for the greater good raise questions about his morality

![suzakupic](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bfa20ec4-a92f-46a1-b223-f9eb5f24a77c)

### Suzaku Kururugi
: A skilled pilot and soldier, Suzaku seeks to change the Britannian Empire from within rather than through violent rebellion. His unwavering sense of justice often puts him at odds with Lelouch's more radical tactics, leading to a complicated friendship marked by both trust and betrayal.

![CC](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/81daf237-409c-4ebb-80bc-29a600f338af)

### C.C.
: A mysterious woman with ties to the power of Geass, C.C. forms a unique bond with Lelouch as both ally and confidante. Her enigmatic past and complex motivations add depth to her character, while her insights into human nature provide a fresh perspective on the events unfolding around her.

![charlespic](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc2a551d-cb96-4864-bd81-ff63fd37688b)

### Charles Di Britannia 
: As the emperor of the Britannian Empire, Charles is a powerful and ruthless ruler whose actions drive much of the story's conflict. His complex relationships with his children, particularly Lelouch and Nunnally, add an emotional dimension to his character that challenges assumptions about his true nature.

<img width="411" height="646" alt="nunnaly png" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09763f40-e7e8-4de6-9753-005bac643fc5" />

### Nunnaly ~~Lamperouge~~ vi Britannia
: Lelouch's younger sister, Nunnally is a gentle and empathetic presence in his life. Despite her physical disabilities, she remains optimistic and determined to create a better world for all people, providing inspiration and motivation for those around her.

![VV](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/45e23612-396d-462e-8ddf-c2cc9aec77ff)

### V.V.
: A boy who, like C.C., possesses the powers of immortality and Geass; his true identity is the twin brother of the Emperor of Britannia. He seems to have had a connection with C.C. and the late Marianne, and after C.C.'s departure, he became the leader of the Geass Order and worked with the Emperor on the plan to "kill God".

![kallenpic](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6337f21e-7185-4db9-bd06-bb63fde79714)
### Kallen Kozuki
: A girl living as Britannian nobility who has immersed herself in illegal anti-Britannian activities. Kallen has an active personality, practices martial arts, and is capable of piloting a Knightmare Frame to an exemplary level. Within Ashford Academy, her double life necessitates she portray herself differently, so she pretends to be frail and sickly.
